# Cois Farraige — Design notes

Taken from the business card and logo. The site is currently a single
"coming soon" page.

## Colour

| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `--plum` | `#523B68` | Text (the card's text colour). 9.6:1 on white |
| `--white` | `#FFFFFF` | Page background |

Other brand colours for later pages:

| Value | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| `#5A2977` | The logo's deep purple. Good for buttons and links (10.4:1 on white) |
| `#BDA9CF` | The card's lavender strip. Decoration and large text only |
| `#D2B5E0` | The logo's outer ring |

## Typography

- **Jost** (Google Fonts) 400, uppercase with `0.3em` letter-spacing for the
  "Website coming soon" line, like "Reflexology & Reiki" on the card.
- For headings on a fuller site later, **Bodoni Moda** is close to the card's
  serif ("Mary Butler").

## Layout

The logo and the title are centred on a white page, with the logo at up to
340px wide (72% of the screen on phones).

## Preferences from Mary's side

- No hamburger menu.
- Don't show the business card on the site.
- No booking buttons or booking forms.
